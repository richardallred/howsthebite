            The score rates conditions, not your odds: water temperature and light count most, then wind, river flow and the moon; in salt water the tide and the surf take the place of flow and moon. Barometric pressure counts least, because studies of angler catch rates have found no effect. Skill, bait and how many fish are there matter more than all of it. USGS readings are provisional.
