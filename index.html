      Where to look

      Picked from where the fish are in their year and the wind right now, then found on the lake's outline from OpenStreetMap: points that reach into the lake, coves and creek arms, the north shore that warms first, and the bank the wind blows into. Rules of thumb, not a fish finder: the outline has no depths, so drop-offs, humps and weed lines can't be shown.
